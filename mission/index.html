§ 03 · Values

Three commitments
that hold the line.

Values aren't decoration. They decide who we work with, how we work, and what we refuse. These are the three we'd defend in any room.

i.
Share skills
and knowledge.
To create long-lasting, sustainable change. Capacity is the only currency that compounds · we transfer it deliberately, every engagement, every time.
ii.
Build collaborative
partnerships.
That promote innovation, growth and impact. Triple-helix by default: government, private sector, academia and non-state actors moving together.
iii.
Lead with integrity.
Commit to safeguarding and take a zero-tolerance approach to abuse and harm. Treat others with respect, as equals. Be open, transparent and accountable in everything we do.
We are not here to deliver reports.
We are here to shift the conditions
that produce the problem.
