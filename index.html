§ 02 · Our approach

Four ways
we work.

Support institutions to be relevant in complex and fast-paced environments. We listen closely, interrogate the process, and bridge sectors to arrive at the best solutions to specific problems.

01 / Make Meaning

Make
meaning

We listen closely, interrogate the process, and bridge sectors to arrive at the best solutions to the problems we face · and the ones that led to them.

02 / Collective Intelligence

Collective
intelligence

A finger on the pulse · what's happening on the ground and what futures are possible. We co-create new approaches to address both the problem and its broader environment.

03 / Portfolio Approach

Portfolio
approach

Co-design, co-create, co-execute. We catalyse partnerships using the triple helix of government, private sector, academia and non-state actors.

04 / Solutions Mapping

Solutions
mapping

Branch out together to explore solutions to the context challenge · mapping options, weighing trade-offs, and committing to the most catalytic path.

§ 02.5 · Inside an engagement

How the
work unfolds.

Every project moves through four motions. They're not a linear waterfall · they overlap, recur, and pull on each other. But they always show up.

PLATE № 01
Fieldwork Process map
Fig. 01·The engagement loop
Motion 01 · Listen

Get close to the
people closest
to the problem.

Before any framework, before any analysis: we sit with the people the work is meant to serve · and the people running it on the ground. We listen for the story underneath the story.

Field visitsStakeholder mappingDiscovery interviews
Motion 02 · Frame

Interrogate process, not just outcomes.

What system produced this problem? What incentives keep it alive? Framing is half the work. We turn complexity into a shared mental model · and a clear bet on where to push.

Theory of ChangeProblem treesSystems mapping
Motion 03 · Co-create

Design with · not
for · the people
who'll live with it.

Triple-helix in motion: government, private sector, academia and non-state actors building the response together. Co-design is not a workshop · it's a discipline.

Co-design sprintsTriple helixPrototype & test
Motion 04 · Hand off

Transfer capacity
so it outlasts us.

The best engagement leaves the organisation with the skills, systems and confidence to run the work itself. We document deliberately. We coach in the open. We leave on time.

Capacity plansCoachingOpen documentation
