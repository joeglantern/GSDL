01

Organisational
Development.

Organizational Capacity Development (OCA) and the subsequent strengthening of organisations through open and bespoke Capacity Improvement plans around prioritised themes.

Governance · Human Resource · Resource Mobilisation · Sustainability · Project Planning & Management · Monitoring & Evaluation · Alliance building & partnership management.

  • 01.a Capacity Improvement plansDeveloped end-to-end, with support in implementation, monitoring and re-evaluation.

  • 01.c Human Resource managementFrom systems and structures to performance and people review.

02

Monitoring, Evaluation,
Research & Learning.

A practice for organisations that want to know · not guess · what works. We instrument programmes so they can learn in motion.

Theory of Change · Logical Frameworks · Baselines · Midlines · Endlines · Operations Research · Reporting.

  • 02.a MEL frameworksDevelopment of Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning frameworks tailored to programme scope and stakeholder needs.
  • 02.b ToC capacityBuilding organizational capacity on Theory of Change based project evaluations and reporting.
  • 02.c Project EvaluationsBaseline, Midline and Endline · across sectors and contexts.
  • 02.d Operations ResearchStructured inquiry on what's actually happening in implementation · and what to change next.
